# Worlds and ground truth

All worlds are generated by [`worlds/make_worlds.py`](../worlds/make_worlds.py), which also writes the ground-truth file
`config/truth_<world>.json` and the settings file `config/<world>.env`. Positions are `(north, east)` metres; the drone starts at the origin and
flies at 2 m. Copy `worlds/*.sdf` into `PX4-Autopilot/Tools/simulation/gz/worlds/` (`environment/patch_px4.sh` does this).

| world | people | scene | purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sage_sar` | 3 standing at (0,4), (−6,7), (5,−6) | open ground, a few visual-only props | baseline |
| `sage_rescue` | 3 standing at (0,5), (−9,9), (10,−9) and **1 walking** on a 12 m path (4.5,−11)↔(4.5,1) at 0.6 m/s | a small town: roads, grass, helipad at the origin, two houses, a wall, trees, bushes, cars, a container | main evaluation and demo scene |
| `sage_hard` | 3 standing + 2 walking | 24 × 24 m, two buildings, a wall, three trees with collision, distractors | stress test |
| `sage_hard_long` | as `sage_hard` with longer walker paths | | motion-visibility study |

Details that matter for evaluation:
- **Search area** ±12 m around the origin for the larger worlds (`config/*.env`); obstacles are listed as rectangles (`SAGE_OBSTACLES`) and inflated by
  the planner (2.5 m in `sage_rescue`) and the manager (1.0 m).
- **Props** in `sage_rescue` are visual-only and lower than the flight altitude; solid obstacles (houses, wall, trees) are in the obstacle map.
- **Scoring rule:** a report is correct if it lies within 1.5 m of a standing person or of a walker's path segment; each true person can be matched
  once (`scripts/score_trial.py`).
- **Drone model:** `models/x500_mono_cam/model.sdf` adds a negligible-mass link carrying a downward camera 5 m above the drone (used only for the
  demo view).
